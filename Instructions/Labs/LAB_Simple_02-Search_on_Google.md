# Simple Lab 02 - Search on Google

## Lab introduction

In this lab, you will learn how to use Google to search for information. Knowing how to use search engines effectively is essential for finding information online.

## Estimated timing: 5 minutes

## Lab scenario

You need to find information about a topic on the internet. You will use Google to search for this information step by step.

## Job skills

+ Task 1: Navigate to Google
+ Task 2: Perform a basic search
+ Task 3: View search results

## Task 1: Navigate to Google

In this task, you will open Google in your browser.

1. Click on the address bar at the top of your browser.

2. Type `https://www.google.com` and press **Enter**.

3. Wait for Google's search page to load completely.

4. You will see the Google homepage with a search box in the center.

## Task 2: Perform a basic search

In this task, you will search for information on Google.

1. Click on the **search box** (the white box with "Search Google" text).

2. Type `cat facts` as your search query.

3. Press **Enter** or click the **Google Search** button.

4. Wait for the search results page to load.

## Task 3: View search results

In this task, you will examine the search results.

1. Observe the list of search results displayed on the page.

2. Each result shows:
   - The website title (in blue or purple)
   - The website URL
   - A brief description of the content

3. Click on the **first search result** to visit that website.

4. Read some of the information about cats on the website.

5. Go back to the search results by clicking the **Back button** in your browser (usually a left arrow at the top left).

## Summary

In this lab, you have successfully:
- Navigated to Google
- Entered a search query
- Viewed search results
- Clicked on a search result to visit a website
- Used the browser's back button to return to search results

These search skills will help you find information about any topic on the internet.
