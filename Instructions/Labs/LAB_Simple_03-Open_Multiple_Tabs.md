# Simple Lab 03 - Open and Manage Multiple Browser Tabs

## Lab introduction

In this lab, you will learn how to open multiple browser tabs and switch between them. Managing multiple tabs allows you to work with several websites at the same time.

## Estimated timing: 5 minutes

## Lab scenario

You want to keep multiple websites open while browsing. You need to open new tabs to view different websites without losing your current page.

## Job skills

+ Task 1: Open a new tab
+ Task 2: Navigate to websites in different tabs
+ Task 3: Switch between tabs
+ Task 4: Close a tab

## Task 1: Open a new tab

In this task, you will open a new browser tab.

1. In your open browser, look at the top where the tabs are displayed.

2. Click on the **"+" icon** next to the current tab to create a new tab.

3. A new blank tab will open with a new address bar ready for input.

**Alternative method:**
- Press **Ctrl + T** on your keyboard to open a new tab instantly.

## Task 2: Navigate to websites in different tabs

In this task, you will navigate to different websites in each tab.

1. In the **first tab**: Make sure you are on `https://www.example.com` (or navigate there).

2. In the **new (second) tab** that is currently active:
   - Click on the address bar
   - Type `https://www.wikipedia.org`
   - Press **Enter** to load Wikipedia

3. Open **another new tab** (press **Ctrl + T**).

4. In the **third tab**:
   - Type `https://www.weather.com` in the address bar
   - Press **Enter** to load a weather website

5. Now you have three tabs open with different websites.

## Task 3: Switch between tabs

In this task, you will switch between the open tabs.

1. Click on the **first tab** (Example.com) to view it.

2. Click on the **second tab** (Wikipedia) to view it.

3. Click on the **third tab** (Weather) to view it.

4. Click back on the **first tab** to return to Example.com.

**Alternative methods:**
- Press **Ctrl + Tab** to switch to the next tab
- Press **Ctrl + Shift + Tab** to switch to the previous tab

## Task 4: Close a tab

In this task, you will close a tab.

1. Right-click on the **weather tab** (or any tab you want to close).

2. Select **Close tab** from the context menu.

3. The tab and its content will close.

**Alternative method:**
- Click the **X button** on the tab itself
- Or press **Ctrl + W** while on the tab you want to close

## Summary

In this lab, you have successfully:
- Opened multiple new tabs
- Navigated to different websites in each tab
- Switched between tabs to view different content
- Closed a tab when finished

Managing multiple tabs allows you to efficiently work with several websites at once without having to navigate back and forth.
