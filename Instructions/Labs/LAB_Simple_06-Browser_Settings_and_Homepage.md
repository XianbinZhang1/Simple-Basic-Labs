# Simple Lab 06 - Explore Browser Settings and Homepage

## Lab introduction

In this lab, you will learn how to access browser settings to customize your browsing experience. You'll explore basic settings like changing your homepage and default search engine.

## Estimated timing: 5 minutes

## Important for Testing

For consistent validation across runs:
- Keep this lab read-only (do not permanently change settings unless explicitly required).
- Validate by locating each settings section, not by exact UI wording.
- Browser wording may differ; section purpose must still match.

## Lab scenario

You want to customize your browser so it opens with your preferred homepage and uses your favorite search engine.

## Job skills

+ Task 1: Open browser settings
+ Task 2: View the current homepage
+ Task 3: Explore other browser settings
+ Task 4: Understand browser preferences

## Task 1: Open browser settings

In this task, you will access the settings menu.

**For Microsoft Edge:**
1. Click the **three dots menu** (⋮) at the top right of the browser.

2. Select **Settings** from the dropdown menu.

**For Google Chrome:**
1. Click the **three dots menu** (⋮) at the top right.

2. Select **Settings**.

**For Firefox:**
1. Click the **menu button** (three horizontal lines) at the top right.

2. Select **Settings**.

3. A new tab will open showing Firefox Settings.

## Task 2: View the current homepage

In this task, you will check what your browser's homepage is set to.

1. In the Settings page, look for **Startup** or **Home** section.

2. You should see options like:
   - "Open the New Tab page" or
   - "Open a specific page or set of pages"

3. Note what your current homepage is set to.

4. You are not required to change it, but now you know where to make changes if needed.

Expected result: you can identify where homepage/startup behavior is configured.

## Task 3: Explore other browser settings

In this task, you will look at other settings categories.

1. Look through the left sidebar for other settings categories like:
   - **Privacy and security** - Controls cookies and data
   - **Search engine** - Choose your default search engine
   - **Languages** - Change browser language
   - **Accessibility** - Font size, contrast options
   - **Appearance** - Theme colors and layout

2. Click on **Search engine** (or similar option).

3. You will see the current default search engine (usually Google, Bing, or similar).

4. You can see a list of available search engines.

5. Return to the main settings by clicking **Settings** or back arrow.

Expected result: you can locate Search engine settings and identify the current default provider.

## Task 4: Understand browser preferences

In this task, you will recognize important preference areas.

1. Look for a **Privacy and security** section.

2. Understand what cookies and cache do (they store website data for faster loading).

3. Note the option to **clear browsing data** - useful for privacy.

4. Look for **Downloads** settings to see where files are saved.

5. Check **Notifications** to control pop-up permissions.

6. You don't need to change anything - just familiarize yourself with where settings are located.

Expected result: you can locate privacy, downloads, and notifications settings sections.

## Summary

In this lab, you have successfully:
- Opened your browser's settings menu
- Located the homepage settings
- Found and reviewed the search engine options
- Explored various browser preferences
- Understood where to customize your browsing experience

Understanding browser settings allows you to personalize your browsing experience and maintain privacy preferences.
